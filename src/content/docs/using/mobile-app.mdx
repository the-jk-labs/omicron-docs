title: Android app
description: Connect the Android app, read posts, interact with writers, and manage your account session.
---

The Android app supports guest reading, signed-in feeds and social actions,
reading lists, a writer dashboard, notifications, and local or federated
profiles. The instance you choose must use HTTPS.

## Choose an instance

1. Open the app and enter your instance address, such as
   `https://omicron.blog`.
2. Select **Continue** after the app confirms the instance.
3. Select **Change instance** at any time to connect to another server.

The app keeps each instance's session separate. Changing instances never sends
an existing session to the new server.

## Sign in or create an account

Use your email address or username and password to sign in. A username may be
entered with or without its leading `@`.

To create an account, select **Create an account** and provide a username,
email address, and password. Usernames use 3 to 30 lowercase letters, numbers,
or underscores. Passwords must be 12 to 128 characters.

If you need another account on the same instance, sign out first, then sign in
with the other account.

## Confirm your email

An instance can require new accounts to confirm their email address. In that
case, the app shows **Check your inbox** after registration or an unverified
sign-in attempt. Open the confirmation link in your email, then return to the
app and sign in.

The confirmation link opens the instance's web verification page. Android App
Links are not currently available for this handoff.

## Navigate the app

The top bar provides **Search** and a theme toggle. When signed in, it also shows
a notification bell with an unread count and an account menu. The bell opens a
recent-notification preview; opening it marks unread notifications as read.
Select **See all** to open the full notifications screen. When signed out, use
the top-bar gear to open Settings.

Signed-in readers also have a bottom navigation bar:

| Destination | Opens                                    |
| ----------- | ---------------------------------------- |
| **Home**    | The For you, Local, and Global feeds     |
| **Lists**   | Your reading lists, including Read later |
| **Write**   | A new post in the composer               |
| **Stats**   | Your writer dashboard                    |
| **Profile** | Your profile                             |

The account menu links to **Profile**, **Write**, **Your posts**, **Lists**,
**Stats**, **Settings**, and **Sign out**. Guests can read the public Home feeds
and use Search, but do not see the signed-in bottom navigation.

## Read posts

After connecting, browse **Global** and **Local** as a guest. When signed in, the
**For you** feed adds posts from writers you follow and posts they recommend.
Select a post's title, summary, or image to read its article, then continue to
related posts below it. Author names open profiles, and topic tags open their
tag pages.

If your session expires while using **For you**, the app switches to **Global**
and shows a sign-in action. After signing in again, select **Continue reading**
to return to the screen you were using.

### Returning to Home

After you have loaded Home, later launches can show its saved feed cards while the
app checks your session and refreshes posts in the background. Saved cards remain
readable during a connection problem; retry the session or feed update from the
message shown on screen. Account actions stay unavailable until the app confirms
your session and loads a fresh feed. A rejected session or feed request removes
the affected saved account cards rather than treating them as current content.

Home data is encrypted on the device and kept separate for the selected instance
and account. It holds up to 30 card previews (within a 1 MiB limit); feed previews
expire after 24 hours and cached account details after 30 days. Signing out clears
the saved Home data. A new installation or a Home feed that has not loaded before
still needs a network connection to show posts. These startup previews are
separate from **Settings → Offline reading** and do not include full article text.

## Interact with posts and comments

Use the actions on a feed card or open a post to **Like**, **Recommend**, or
**Save for later**. Recommendations are reposts that federate to followers; see
[Recommending](/using/reading/#recommending). **Save for later** adds the post
to your private [Read later list](/using/lists/#read-later). Select an active
action again to unlike, remove the recommendation, or remove the saved post.

### Responses and replies

1. Open an article and select its comment-count action to move to **Responses**.
2. Sign in, then enter a response in **What are your thoughts?** and select
   **Respond**. Responses are plain text, support multiple lines, and can contain
   up to 2,000 characters. Wait for the initial responses list to finish loading
   before submitting.
3. Select **Reply** on a comment to move to the response composer. It shows
   **Replying to** the selected author and focuses the reply field. Select
   **Respond** to send the reply, or **Cancel reply** to return to a top-level
   response. Changing or cancelling the reply target keeps the unfinished text.

While sending, the field and reply-target controls are disabled. The new response
shows **Posting…** and has no action controls until the server confirms it. The
field clears after a successful submission. Replies to replies stay in the same
top-level thread.

Select a response's heart to like it; select the active heart again to unlike it.
Your own responses also offer **Edit** and **Delete**. After editing, select
**Save**. Deleting asks for confirmation. Deleting a top-level response also
removes its replies and cannot be undone.

### Failed actions and retries

If an action fails, the app restores only that action's previous state and count;
another successful like or recommendation stays active. Article errors appear
beside the affected action, comment, or editor.

| Feedback                  | Recovery                                                                               |
| ------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Offline or failed request | Restore your connection if needed, then select **Try again** beside the failed action. |
| Expired session           | Select **Sign in**, authenticate again, and return to the article.                     |
| Action not allowed        | The account is not permitted to perform the action; the error has no retry button.     |

A failed response keeps its text and reply target so **Try again** sends it to
the same thread. A failed edit keeps the edited text ready to save again. Retrying
a failed deletion asks for confirmation again. **Try again** for a failed Read
later save or removal repeats that action.

Response drafts and retry state are held in memory. They are not saved as offline
drafts or queued to send automatically when connectivity returns.

## Open profiles

Select a writer's name on a post, or **Profile** in the account menu or bottom
navigation, to open a profile. Profiles include **Articles**,
**Recommendations**, and **About**.
On an unlocked local profile, the **Followers** and **Following** counts open a
separate people screen. Switch between the counted tabs, then select a person's
avatar or name to open their profile. Remote profiles show the follower and
following counts available from the remote instance, but do not have member
lists. A private local profile keeps its counts visible, but its member lists
stay hidden until you are an approved follower.

Each person in a member list has a **Follow** action. Sign in to follow someone;
the button then shows **Following** or, for a private local account, **Requested**
until the writer approves you. Selecting **Following** asks you to confirm before
unfollowing. Cancelling a pending **Requested** follow also asks for confirmation.
On your own Following list, a successful follow or unfollow updates the list and
count. Open **More actions** (the ellipsis button) on another writer's profile to
mute or block them. A block also ends the follow relationship. Links in **About**
open outside the app. Remote profiles and actions require federation to be enabled
on the instance.

If a local profile is private, you can still see its public header and counts, but
its posts and member lists stay hidden until you are an approved follower.

## Write and manage posts

Sign in, then select **Write** in the bottom navigation or account menu to open a
new post.
The composer supports a title and body blocks (paragraphs, headings, lists,
quotes, code, and images), plus an optional description, tags, language, and
banner. Save a draft, publish now, or schedule it for a later time. Scheduled
times use your phone's timezone.

The title and body are the main writing area. Use the **Add block** toolbar below
the document to insert a paragraph, heading, quote, code block, list, divider, or
image; each tool has its own icon and label. A new text block opens ready for
typing. Use the block's **More actions** menu to remove it.

Select **Post details** to add an optional cover, description, tags, or language.
You can collapse this section while writing; its values stay in the post. **Save
draft** and **Publish** stay above the document. To schedule a post, open
**Publishing options** beside **Publish** and select **Schedule**. While the
keyboard is open, the block toolbar remains above it and the bottom navigation
is hidden.

To manage posts, open **Your posts** in the account menu or select **Manage posts**
on your own profile.
The screen has **Drafts**, **Scheduled**, and **Published** tabs with a count on
each. Select **Load more** when a list has another page.

| Tab           | Available actions                                                                                                 |
| ------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Drafts**    | Continue editing, schedule, or delete. Deleting asks for confirmation.                                            |
| **Scheduled** | Continue editing, reschedule, publish now, unschedule back to a draft, or delete. Deleting asks for confirmation. |
| **Published** | View, edit, unpublish back to a draft, or delete. Unpublish and delete ask for confirmation.                      |

Drafts and scheduled posts autosave after you pause editing. Editing a scheduled
post this way keeps its scheduled time; selecting **Save draft** explicitly
unschedules it.
Published edits are not autosaved: select **Save changes** to update the live
post. It remains published, and the update is queued for remote followers.
Unpublishing makes the post a draft and sends a deletion to remote followers, but
remote servers are not guaranteed to remove every copy.

## Reading lists

Select **Lists** in the bottom navigation or account menu to open your lists.
**Read later** is pinned at the top. Select **New list** to create a public or
private list, then select a list to browse its articles. Longer lists use **Load
more** to retrieve the next page.

The bookmark action on a feed card or article adds or removes it from **Read
later**. Open **Settings → Offline reading** to manage public articles cached on
the device; offline copies are separate from reading lists.

## Writer dashboard

Select **Stats** in the bottom navigation or account menu to open your dashboard.
It shows aggregate views when the instance enables view counting, plus likes,
comments, engagement, followers, a views-over-time chart, and per-post totals.
The dashboard reports aggregate counts only; it does not identify readers.
When view counting is disabled, the views metrics are omitted.

## Notifications

The signed-in top-bar bell shows the unread count and a recent-notification
preview. Opening the preview or the full screen marks notifications as read.
Select **See all** to open the full list. Select a notification to open the
related article or writer profile; use **Load more** to fetch older
notifications.

## Settings

When signed out, select the top-bar gear. When signed in, open **Settings** from
the account menu. The screen remains available when signed out.

| Setting             | What it does                                                                                                                                                                                                     |
| ------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Instance**        | Shows the connected instance. Select **Change instance** to open the connection screen, then choose another address there.                                                                                       |
| **Appearance**      | Choose **System**, **Light**, or **Dark**. The preference is saved on this device across restarts and instance changes. **System** follows Android's current appearance. It is not synced with your web account. |
| **About Omicron**   | Identifies the app and its license.                                                                                                                                                                              |
| **Offline reading** | Opens public articles saved on this device for the selected instance.                                                                                                                                            |
| **Account**         | Shows the signed-in account and provides **Sign out**, or offers **Sign in** when signed out.                                                                                                                    |

Signing out clears the saved session on this device even when the instance cannot
confirm the remote sign-out. In that case, Settings displays a warning.

### Offline reading

When you open a public article online, the app saves its article details on the
device. Open **Settings → Offline reading** to see saved articles for the
currently selected instance. Select **Read** to open one, **Remove** to remove
an individual copy, or **Clear saved articles for this instance** to remove all
of that instance's copies. Switching instances does not mix or clear their
saved articles.

Every article open checks the instance first. If a network connection fails, the
app can show the saved copy, marked as possibly out of date. A server response
such as **Not found** or a sign-in requirement is not replaced with cached content.
This article library does not save drafts, scheduled posts, feed pages, comments,
or account-specific likes and recommendations. The separate Home startup cache
contains only the limited card previews described above.

Copies expire 30 days after their last online update. Across instances, the
device cache is limited to 50 articles and 64 MB total; it removes the
least-recently read copies first. Images use a separate 32 MB cache. An image
may be missing if it was not downloaded before or has been evicted, and Android
may clear that cache at any time.

## Accessibility

The Android reading and Settings screens support TalkBack and follow Android's
font scale. Enable TalkBack in Android's accessibility settings to navigate
controls by focus and hear their labels and selected states. Top-bar actions,
account-menu items, bottom-navigation tabs, and article controls have accessible
labels. The **For you**, **Local**, and **Global** scopes and bottom tabs expose
their selected state. In Settings, **System**, **Light**, and **Dark** are radio
choices with the selected appearance exposed to TalkBack. **Like**,
**Recommend**, and **Save for later** expose whether each action is selected.
Comment hearts expose their like count and selected state. Comment action rows
wrap on narrow screens and at larger font scales, with 48dp minimum touch targets.

Full-screen loading states keep progress centered and show a message describing
what is loading. TalkBack exposes the same message on the progress indicator.

Article headings are available for heading navigation. Inline article images use
their supplied description when one is available; cover images and decorative
images are not announced.

## Session security

The app stores the session cookie securely on the device and only uses it with
the instance that issued it. API access tokens remain in memory and are renewed
before they expire. If the instance rejects a session, its saved cookie is cleared
and the app offers a way to sign in again. Signing out removes the current
in-memory session and asks the instance to end its session.
